# ChangeNotify Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

ChangeNotify shows you a notification when you are mentioned, assigned, or asked to review something on GitHub.

## What the app accesses
- Your GitHub notifications and, for pull requests that notify you, their status (for example "checks failing" or "ready to merge"). This is retrieved from GitHub's API using the access token you authorize.

## What is stored, and where
- Your GitHub access token is stored **only in your device's Keychain**.
- A list of notification IDs already shown is stored **on your device** so each alert appears once.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** ChangeNotify has no server, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your token, your notifications or any other data. The app communicates only with GitHub (github.com and api.github.com).

## Permissions
The app asks permission to show notifications. It requests only the GitHub `notifications` scope.

## Revoking access
Sign out in the app to delete the token from your device. You can also revoke ChangeNotify at any time at https://github.com/settings/applications.

## Children
ChangeNotify is not directed at children.

## Changes and contact
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/JimGBritt/change-notify/issues.
